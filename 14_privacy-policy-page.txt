--- POLICY SECTIONS ---

Section 1: Sovereign Data Isolation & Storage
  "All documents submitted to DOCKEXPERT are processed inside MeitY-empanelled cloud data centers within the Republic of India or on-premise within the institution's designated State Data Center (SDC). No institutional data or extracted OCR text is ever transmitted overseas or utilized to train general foundation models."

Section 2: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Compliance
  "Faculty Aadhaar numbers, personal bank account details, and private contact info are subjected to automatic client-side or ingestion-time redacting filters. Only authorized Scrutiny Officers with verified digital identities are granted cryptographic authorization to inspect evidence bounding boxes."
  
  Compliance Points:
    - Automated PII masking for government identity numbers.
    - Role-based access control (RBAC) enforced with multi-factor authentication.
    - Data minimization: OCR tokens not required for APH compliance are purged within 72 hours.

Section 3: Immutable Append-Only Audit Logging
  "To prevent retroactive alterations and regulatory fraud, every verification event, officer decision, and file re-upload is cryptographically sealed into an append-only ledger with SHA-256 hash anchoring. Audit records cannot be deleted or overwritten by any administrative user."

Section 4: Institutional Control & Deletion Rights
  "Colleges and technical institutions maintain full data sovereignty. Once formal AICTE Approval / EoA proceedings are completed, institutions may request archival or purge of non-statutory scratch data in accordance with regulatory retention mandates."

Section 5: Data Retention Policy
  Active Scrutiny Period: Documents retained for duration of AICTE review cycle
  Post-Approval Archive: 7 years (as per regulatory retention mandate)
  Rejected Applications: Metadata retained 3 years; documents purged on institution request
  PII (Aadhaar, Bank details): Masked at ingestion; never stored in plaintext

Section 6: Security Standards
  - Encryption at rest: AES-256
  - Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3
  - Certificate management: Let's Encrypt + internal PKI
  - Penetration testing: Quarterly (CERT-In empanelled firm)
  - ISO 27001:2022 certified

Section 7: Contact for Privacy Concerns
  Data Protection Officer: dpo@dockexpert.gov.in
  Grievance Redressal: Within 30 days as per DPDP Act §13
  Regulatory Authority: MeitY DPDP Compliance Cell, New Delhi
