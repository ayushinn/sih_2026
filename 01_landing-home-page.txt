Section Heading: "How DOCKEXPERT Works"

Step 1 — Institution Applicant:
  Title: Upload Document Bundle
  Description: Institutions upload their complete AICTE document bundle — Fire NOCs, land titles, faculty rosters, FDRs — via the secure pre-check workspace.

Step 2 — AI Engine:
  Title: AI Extraction & Cross-Check
  Description: The OCR engine extracts structured data from every document, cross-reconciles values, and flags APH non-compliances with clause citations.
