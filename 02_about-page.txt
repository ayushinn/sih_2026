--- TECHNOLOGY STACK SECTION ---
Badge: Under The Hood
Heading: "Technology That Powers DOCKEXPERT"

Tech Item 1:
  Title: Multi-Layer OCR Pipeline
  Description: Combines Tesseract, PaddleOCR, and a fine-tuned transformer model for 97%+ accuracy on government-issued certificates, architectural drawings, and handwritten affidavits.

Tech Item 2:
  Title: Cross-Document Reconciliation Engine
  Description: Parses and correlates structured entities (names, dates, acreage values, FDR amounts) across 25+ document types using NLP-based entity matching.

Tech Item 3:
  Title: Cryptographic Audit Ledger
  Description: SHA-256 hash anchoring with append-only WORM (Write Once Read Many) storage. Every event is sealed with a Merkle proof for tamper-evidence.

--- TEAM / SIH CONTEXT SECTION ---
Badge: SIH 2026 — Problem #1702
Heading: "Built for Smart India Hackathon"

Description:
  DOCKEXPERT is the winning prototype submission for SIH 2026 Problem Statement #1702 — "AI-Based Document Scrutiny System for AICTE Institutional Approvals." Our team comprises final-year engineering students from Pune, Maharashtra, specializing in AI, full-stack development, and regulatory compliance.

Problem Statement Summary:
  AICTE receives thousands of institutional approval applications annually. Manual scrutiny is time-consuming, error-prone, and susceptible to fraudulent document submissions. The challenge: build an AI system that automates multi-document verification while keeping regulatory officers in control of final decisions.

Our Solution Highlights:
  - Automated OCR extraction from 25+ AICTE-mandated document types
  - Real-time cross-reconciliation with APH clause citations
  - Human-in-the-loop decision recording with justification notes
  - Immutable blockchain-anchored audit trail
  - Cryptographic QR stamping for approved documents
  - Enterprise-grade on-premise deployment for regulatory offices
