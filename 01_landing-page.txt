--- WORKFLOW SECTION ---
Label: WORKFLOW SIMULATION
Heading: "Regulatory Scrutiny Lifecycle"
Sub: "Step-by-step pre-check validation and officer scrutiny workflow"

Steps:
  Step 1: "Institution Uploads Document Package"
    "Applicant uploads digital certificates for fire safety, land, faculty roster, and society registration in PDF, JPG, or PNG format."

  Step 2: "Async Validation Job Enqueued (HTTP 202)"
    "Backend returns a tracked job identifier; client polls with backoff until background OCR and verification concludes."

  Step 3: "OCR & Layout Analysis Extracts Metadata"
    "Spatial text extraction identifies validity dates, sanctioned intake numbers, land survey plots, and building areas."

  Step 4: "Rule Engine Evaluates Compliance Norms & Expiry"
    "Cross-references statutory regulatory guidelines and approval norms. Flags high-severity expiry and ratio deviations."

  Step 5: "Evidence-Linked Advisory Findings Generated"
    "Surfaces findings with model confidence, exact page and coordinate bounding references, and regulatory clause citations."

  Step 6: "Authorised Officer Reviews & Records Decision"
    "Officer reviews side-by-side evidence, enters mandatory statutory justification notes, and certifies regulatory outcome."
