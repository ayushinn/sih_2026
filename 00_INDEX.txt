ROLES IN THE SYSTEM
=============================================================

1. Applicant Institution
   - Uploads documents, runs pre-check, rectifies flagged issues
   - Tracks application status through scrutiny pipeline

2. Scrutiny Officer
   - Reviews scrutiny queue
   - Examines bounding-box evidence
   - Accepts or dismisses findings
   - Records statutory decisions with mandatory justification notes

3. Verification & Inspection Team
   - Verifies certificates against external portals
   - Conducts manual verifier confirmation workflows

4. Administrator
   - Maintains versioned handbook checklists
   - Configures rules and manages user roles
   - Has NO decision-making power

5. Auditor
   - Inspects tamper-evident, append-only immutable audit trail
   - Reviews compliance verification records
